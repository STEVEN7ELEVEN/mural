## 🎯 What Is mural?

mural is a language-learning app for your iPhone that throws away the boring flashcards, repetitive drills, and meaningless word lists. Instead, you learn by having real conversations. Think of it as a friendly chat partner that lives in your pocket, speaks your target language, and guides you through natural dialogue. It’s the app you download when you want to actually *speak* a new language — and eventually, you’ll delete it because you won’t need it anymore.

Whether you’re a beginner fumbling through greetings or someone who wants to polish their accent, mural adapts to your level and keeps the conversation flowing. It’s open source, free, and built with love using SwiftUI for iOS.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 💬 Conversation-First Learning
- No grammar drills. No word matching games. You talk, and mural responds.
- Lessons are built around everyday scenarios: ordering coffee, asking for directions, making small talk, or expressing opinions.
- The app gently corrects your mistakes without breaking your flow.

### 🧠 Adaptive Difficulty
- mural watches your progress and adjusts the complexity of conversations.
- Start with simple phrases and gradually move to idiomatic expressions and complex sentence structures.
- It tracks which words and phrases you struggle with, then weaves them into future chats.

### 🎙️ Voice-Powered Interaction
- Speak naturally using your iPhone’s microphone. mural listens and replies out loud.
- Practice your pronunciation and get instant feedback on your accent.
- Perfect for commuters, joggers, or anyone who prefers talking over typing.

### 📚 Vocabulary in Context
- Forget memorizing lists. Every new word is introduced within a meaningful conversation.
- Swipe left on any phrase to see its translation, usage notes, and example sentences.
- Your personal “word bank” is automatically built from the phrases you actually use.

### 🔓 100% Open Source
- Built with SwiftUI, mural is fully open source. That means no hidden trackers, no corporate agenda, no pay-to-win features.
- You’re free to inspect the code, contribute improvements, or customize it to your own needs.

### 🛡️ Privacy-First
- Your conversations stay on your device. No cloud processing, no accounts, no data harvesting.
- mural works entirely offline once downloaded, so you can practice anywhere without worrying about your data.

## ✅ Frequently Asked Questions

### ❓ Is mural free?
Yes, 100% free. No in-app purchases, no premium tiers, no ads. It’s open-source software built by a community of passionate developers.

### ❓ Do I need an account?
No. mural is fully local. You don’t need to register, log in, or provide any personal information.

### ❓ Does mural work without Wi-Fi?
Yes! Once downloaded, all language data and conversation features are stored on your phone. You can practice anywhere — on a plane, in a subway tunnel, or in the middle of a forest.

### ❓ What languages can I learn?
mural supports 10 major languages including Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Italian, Portuguese, Mandarin Chinese, Russian, and English (for speakers of other languages). More languages are planned.

### ❓ I’m a total beginner. Will this work for me?
Absolutely. mural starts with basic greetings and simple phrases. You can choose your level during setup, and the app will guide you from there. The key is just to start talking.

### ❓ Can I change the pace?
Yes, you can slow down or speed up conversations at any time. There’s also a “transcript mode” that shows you the text of the conversation, with tap-to-hear pronunciation.
